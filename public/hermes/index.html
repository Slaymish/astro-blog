Hermes Agent

A personal automation agent that runs on one person's own computer.

Hermes Agent is a private, single-user software agent. It is not a product, has no sign-up, and is not offered to the public. It runs locally on the laptop of its owner, Hamish Burke, in Wellington, New Zealand, and acts only on his behalf and only on his own accounts and devices.

What it does

  • Reads and drafts email, and sends messages that Hamish has asked it to send.
  • Reads his calendar and creates or moves events when he asks.
  • Reads and writes his own documents, spreadsheets and task lists.
  • Keeps a local, private record of his finances from data he already has access to.
  • Runs scheduled reminders and summaries for him.

How it works

The agent runs on the same machine as its owner and stores its working data there. It talks to Google services through the OAuth grant that Hamish gives it himself, and only for the scopes he approves on the consent screen. It is not a hosted service, has no servers of its own holding user data, and no third party is given access to anything it can see.

Privacy

What it can see, how it is stored and how to withdraw access are described in the privacy policy.

# Portfolio value ranking

All 52 published catalogue entries: 46 works, 3 posts, 3 reports. No drafts were returned.

Editorial portfolio significance, not measured quality. Scores are judgement calls, not probabilities.

0..100 in five-point steps. 90..100 flagship; 75..85 substantial; 60..70 solid; 45..55 focused/limited; 0..40 exploratory or weak evidence.

Assessed from the existing case studies, posts and the three report PDFs. This is not a fresh source-code or reproducibility audit. AI assistance itself is not penalised; shallow integration, limited evidence and incomplete delivery are. A small piece can have real personal or creative value while carrying less weight as evidence of professional capability.

Scores attach to individual entries. Project/report pairs describe the same work and must not be counted twice in aggregate portfolio evidence. Homepage selection and work-index ordering use the hidden value-plus-recency policy documented in README.md. Chronological feed consumers remain chronological.

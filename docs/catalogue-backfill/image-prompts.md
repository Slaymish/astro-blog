## Otto replacement, 10 October 2026

Generated with built-in imagegen following the explicit request to replace the app-icon cover. Prompt: Create a wide landscape portfolio cover illustration for Otto, a native Swift voice and text assistant for the Mac. Use an elegant tactile editorial still life: a slim aluminium desktop display on a warm pale stone desk, with a small microphone at the left. A restrained luminous cobalt-blue waveform curves from the microphone towards three small physical capability cards and then into floating translucent window shapes near the display, expressing voice requests becoming desktop actions. A neat stack of small session notes sits beside the cards to suggest learning from previous sessions. These are clearly sculptural, conceptual objects, not a screenshot or a claimed recreation of Otto's interface. Warm paper, charcoal, brushed aluminium, soft natural daylight, subtle shadows, ample breathing room, polished contemporary composition. Wide 16:9 framing, objects fill the centre and lower half, attractive at thumbnail scale. No readable text, no logos, no app icon, no giant glowing ring, no people, no private information, no claims of measured performance.
